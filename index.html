★ Flagship · AI Tools

AI Spotify Playlist Maker

Type a vibe in plain language — "rainy afternoon lo-fi with a bit of jazz" — and get a real Spotify playlist with real tracks added to your account.

Turns natural language into structured artist and genre queries with Gemini, then writes straight to a live third-party account via the Spotify Web API. Tkinter UI with multithreading so the app doesn't freeze mid-request.

02 / About

A bit about me

I'm a Computer Science (Honors) student at the University at Albany, SUNY, with a minor in Machine Learning Informatics. I write code because I like the moment when someone I don't know installs a thing I made and it just works.

I did Stanford's Code in Place and AWS's Intro to Generative AI, and made the Dean's List in Fall 2025. What that adds up to: I can pick up a new stack fast, I ship, and I know enough about AI to use it without pretending it's magic.

I grew up in a pastor's family in Nepal and taught a teen Sunday School group in Lalitpur before I moved to the US. That's where I first learned that a good tool is one that meets people where they are — and it's why I want to build things for other international students who are figuring out a new country the way I am now.
